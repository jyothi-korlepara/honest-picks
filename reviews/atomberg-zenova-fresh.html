What makes it different

Most mixer grinders do the same job in roughly the same way. The Zenova Fresh stands out for three things I haven't found on other mixers.

Fresh juice without crushed seeds

Soft fruits like watermelon and citrus juice well in the coarse mode. The seeds aren't crushed, and the juice tastes good.

A locking lid, so you can walk away

The lid locks in place while the mixer runs. You don't have to stand at the mixer holding the jar lid down, and you can get on with something else while it works.

Chutneys the way they should be

Coarse mode makes chutneys very efficiently compared to other mixer grinders. You get the coarse, silbatta-style texture instead of a smooth paste.

What's good

  • Coarse mode leaves seeds uncrushed, and fruit juice tastes good
